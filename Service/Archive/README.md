# Archive

Finished development rounds. These are the goal specs the work was done
against and the evidence it was accepted on, kept because the repository's
argument for trusting a receipt rests on being able to read how earlier claims
were checked.

They are **history, not instructions**. Anything still binding lives in
[`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md); anything still true about the product lives in
[`README.md`](../../README.md) and `docs/`. Where the two disagree, the archive
is the one that is out of date.

Test and source docstrings cite these by path, which is why they were archived
rather than deleted. Later cycles are in [`../Handoffs`](../Handoffs),
[`../Audits`](../Audits) and [`../Research`](../Research).
